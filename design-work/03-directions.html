CrossPay: three visual directions

Each column shows the palette (contrast against the card surface in brackets), the type scale, the button set (press the buttons), badges and a receipt card, in light and dark. Scores and the full write-up are in 03-directions.md.

Direction A · recommended

Signed Ledger

Paper and ink. A light-first statement look: warm paper background, ink text, one verdigris signal colour, amounts in tabular mono figures. Controls are tactile (a 3 px pressable edge, softly embossed icon tiles) but the page itself is flat and calm. Motion explains money moving: connectors draw between steps, figures tick once.

Direction B

Control Room

Dark-first and technical. Graphite surfaces, a cyan signal and lime focus, dense tables, monospace everywhere numbers appear. Flat controls with inner hairlines, no depth. Motion is fast (150 ms) and status-driven: pulses on live items, a scan on the feed.
