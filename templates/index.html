Three outcomes, one rule set

Thresholds come from configuration; the model's class can only make the outcome stricter.

{{ tile('check', 'success') }}

Approve

Score below the review threshold. The settlement is mined into a block and signed into the audit chain in one database transaction.

{{ tile('pause', 'warning') }}

Hold

A compliance officer reviews the evidence, optionally with an AI copilot, writes a reason in their own words, and releases or rejects.

{{ tile('ban', 'danger') }}

Block

High score or a HIGH model class. Nothing is mined; the decision itself is still signed into the audit chain.
